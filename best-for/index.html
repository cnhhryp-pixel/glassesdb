Use-case hub

Choose by job,
not by hype.

The right smart glasses depend heavily on what you expect them to do.

Everyday AI

For calls, audio, reminders, assistants and occasional hands-free capture.

Travel

Look for low weight, translation, navigation assistance and all-day comfort.

Gaming

Prioritize display quality, latency, device compatibility and comfort.

Movies

Display size, image quality and source-device compatibility matter most.

Work

Consider text readability, notifications, portable-screen workflows and enterprise support.

Hands-free capture

Camera placement, stabilization, privacy indicators and sharing workflow are key.

There is no universal “best” pair

A strong camera-and-audio product may be the wrong choice for someone who wants a private virtual monitor. The Finder uses your priorities to narrow the database instead of forcing one global ranking.
