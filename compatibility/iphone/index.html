Compatibility guide

Smart glasses for iPhone

There are two very different compatibility paths: Bluetooth/app-based smart glasses and display glasses that need video output from USB-C.

App-based AI glasses

Ray-Ban Meta, Even G2, Halliday G2 and Solos models pair through companion apps and Bluetooth. Check iOS version and regional feature availability.

Display glasses

USB-C iPhones with DisplayPort video output are the simplest route for XREAL, RayNeo and Rokid display glasses. Older Lightning iPhones generally need additional adapters and may have limitations.

RayNeo

RayNeo officially lists iPhone 15 and iPhone 16 series support for Air 3s Pro, with direct USB-C display connection on compatible models.

XREAL / Rokid

For display glasses, confirm that the iPhone outputs video over USB-C and that any required adapter is supported by the glasses maker.

Simple rule

If the glasses need a video signal, Bluetooth alone is not enough. If they are AI/audio glasses without a display, the companion app and OS version matter more than DisplayPort.
