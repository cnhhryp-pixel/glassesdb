Gaming compatibility

Smart glasses for Nintendo Switch

Switch is less direct than Steam Deck. In practice, most display glasses need a dock, hub or brand-specific accessory to turn the Switch's video output into a usable glasses connection.

XREAL

XREAL states that Nintendo Switch compatibility requires an XREAL Hub or supported dock/accessory rather than a simple direct cable.

Why direct USB-C often fails

The Switch's USB-C behavior is not the same as a laptop or Steam Deck DisplayPort output. Treat the dock/adapter as part of the setup.
