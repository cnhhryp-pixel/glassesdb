Compatibility guide

Smart glasses for Android

Android compatibility depends heavily on the exact phone model. USB-C does not automatically mean the phone can output video.

Check USB-C video output

Display glasses usually require DisplayPort Alt Mode over USB-C. Some Android flagships support it; many mid-range phones do not.

Check the official support list

RayNeo, XREAL and Rokid maintain device compatibility guidance. Use the exact phone model rather than assuming all Android devices behave the same.

AI glasses

Ray-Ban Meta, Even, Halliday and Solos typically rely on Bluetooth and a companion app instead of direct video output.

Desktop modes can help

Phones with desktop-display modes can be especially useful with wearable displays, but support varies by device and software version.
