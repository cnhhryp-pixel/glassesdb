Translation glasses fall into two groups: display models that show translated text in your field of view, and audio/camera models that translate through a phone or assistant without a visual overlay.

Display or audio?

If you want private subtitles, choose a display model. If hearing the translation is enough, camera/audio AI glasses may be simpler and lighter.

Internet still matters

Many translation features use a phone and cloud service. Some brands support selected offline language packs, but offline capability should be checked per language and software version.

What to compare for translation

Supported languages, one-way vs two-way translation, text-display readability, microphone pickup, internet dependence, privacy, regional availability and battery life matter more than generic AI branding.
