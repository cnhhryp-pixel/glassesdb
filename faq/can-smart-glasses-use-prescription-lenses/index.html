FAQ

Can smart glasses use prescription lenses?

Many can. The important detail is how the prescription is implemented.

Prescription-ready frames

Some smart glasses are sold like ordinary optical frames and can be configured with prescription lenses. Meta currently sells prescription-ready Ray-Ban Meta styles, with supported prescription ranges varying by product and retailer.

Prescription inserts

Many display glasses use a small prescription insert mounted behind the main optical system. This is common when the display optics themselves cannot simply be replaced by standard lenses.

Built-in diopter adjustment

Some display glasses include adjustable myopia correction. This can reduce the need for inserts within the supported diopter range but is not equivalent to a full prescription for every user.

Optician-compatible frames

Rokid documents an official magnetic prescription frame that can be taken to a local optical store, subject to lens-shape and curvature requirements.

What to verify before ordering

Check sphere, cylinder, axis, PD, progressive-lens support, whether the prescription is integrated or inserted, and whether third-party lens fitting affects the warranty.
