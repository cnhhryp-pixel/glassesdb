FAQ

Are smart glasses good for gaming?

Display glasses can be. Camera-first AI glasses generally are not gaming displays.

Best fit: display glasses

XREAL, RayNeo and Rokid display models can act like a private large screen for handheld PCs, phones and computers.

Compatibility comes first

The source device must output video in a format the glasses can accept. XREAL, for example, requires USB-C DisplayPort output for direct connection on supported devices.

Refresh rate matters

120 Hz support can make gaming smoother, but the source device, cable, adapter and game must all support the desired output mode.

Switch is a special case

Nintendo Switch typically requires a dock/hub or brand-specific adapter rather than the simple direct USB-C connection used by Steam Deck.

What to compare for gaming

Field of view, refresh rate, weight, comfort, source-device compatibility, charging while playing and whether spatial anchoring works without extra hardware.
