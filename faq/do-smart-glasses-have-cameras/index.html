FAQ

Do all smart glasses have cameras?

No. Camera presence is one of the clearest ways to separate smart-glasses categories.

Camera-first AI glasses

Ray-Ban Meta, Rokid Glasses and Solos AirGo V use cameras for hands-free photos/video and visual AI workflows.

Camera-free HUD glasses

Even G2, Halliday G2 and Vuzix Z100 are examples of glasses designed around in-view information without an outward-facing camera.

Wearable display glasses

XREAL One Pro, RayNeo Air 3s Pro and Rokid Max 2 primarily act as displays and do not use a forward-facing camera in their standard display role.

Privacy tradeoff

No camera does not mean no sensors, microphones, Bluetooth or cloud services. Review the full data path rather than relying only on the camera specification.
