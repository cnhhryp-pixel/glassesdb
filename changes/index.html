GlassesDB change log

Major changes to the database, comparison logic and research structure are recorded here. Small copy edits are not listed individually.

September 28, 2026 — Extended specifications

Added verified extended fields for selected high-interest models, including resolution, field of view, refresh rate, brightness, battery, wireless, storage, IP rating and camera details where official sources provide them. Missing fields remain blank rather than inferred.

September 28, 2026 — Shareable comparisons

The comparison tool now supports preselected model IDs through URL parameters a, b and c. Selected models are also written back into the URL when the user changes a comparison.

September 28, 2026 — Research layer

Added compatibility guides, question-based FAQ pages, use-case guides and model-vs-model comparison pages to connect database records with common buying and research questions.

September 28, 2026 — Database expansion

Expanded the initial database from 8 demonstration entries to 33 tracked models across 10 brand families and added Current / Legacy status labels.

Source policy

When a field is time-sensitive or unclear, GlassesDB prefers an explicit “not listed,” “varies,” approximate label, or blank field over unsupported precision. See the full source methodology.
