2026 category guide

AI glasses are now
three different product types.

The useful question is not “which AI glasses are best?” but whether you need a camera, a display, both, or neither. Start with the job.

Camera-first AI glasses

Examples include Ray-Ban Meta Gen 2, Oakley Meta models and Solos AirGo V. They prioritize hands-free capture, audio and visual AI.

Browse AI glasses →

Display + AI glasses

Models such as RayNeo X3 Pro and Rokid Glasses combine visual information with camera or assistant features.

View RayNeo X3 Pro →

Camera-free AI display glasses

Even G2 and Halliday G2 emphasize translation, prompts, teleprompter and notifications without an outward-facing camera.

Compare Even G2 and Halliday G2 →

Audio-first AI glasses

Display-free, camera-free designs can still provide voice assistants, calls and open-ear audio with a more conventional appearance.

View Solos AirGo 3 →

What to compare before buying

Check display type, camera presence, regional AI availability, microphone/audio design, prescription support, battery expectations and whether core features depend on a phone or cloud connection.
