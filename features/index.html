Display

A display can mean a subtle heads-up text layer or a large virtual screen. Field of view, brightness and optical design matter as much as whether a display exists.

Camera

Cameras enable hands-free photo, video and visual AI, but change privacy considerations and usually add power demands.

Open-ear audio

Speakers in the temples can handle calls, music and assistants without blocking the ear canal, but sound leakage varies.

AI assistant

AI capability may live partly on the glasses, on a paired phone, or in the cloud. Availability can differ by region and language.

Prescription support

Some frames support prescription lenses directly, others require inserts, and some are not designed for prescription use. Verify with the manufacturer or optician.

Compatibility

Display glasses often need USB-C DisplayPort output. Other smart glasses pair through a companion app. “Works with phones” is not specific enough.
