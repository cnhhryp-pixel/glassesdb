Some smart glasses replace your normal prescription frames. Others use inserts, optional lens partners or built-in diopter adjustment. Compare the optical path before the electronics.

Checklist before ordering

Confirm sphere and cylinder range, PD requirements, whether progressive lenses are supported, whether the prescription is integrated or inserted, and whether replacing the prescription later requires replacing the smart frame.

Prescription availability and supported ranges can vary by country, seller and lens partner.
