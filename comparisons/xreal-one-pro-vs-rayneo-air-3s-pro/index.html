Where XREAL One Pro differs

XREAL One Pro integrates the X1 spatial-computing chip, supports native 3DoF display behavior, and has a wider 57° field of view. It is the more spatially oriented architecture of the two.

XREAL One Pro details →

Where RayNeo Air 3s Pro differs

RayNeo emphasizes high perceived brightness, 120 Hz gaming, 76 g weight and broad entertainment-device compatibility through adapters.

RayNeo Air 3s Pro details →

Which type of buyer should compare these?

If your priority is a portable large screen for games and movies, both belong on the same shortlist. If native spatial anchoring and a wider field of view are important, inspect XREAL's spatial features closely. If lower weight and a straightforward entertainment setup matter more, RayNeo's design may fit your workflow better.
