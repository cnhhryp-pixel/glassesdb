Choose the category first

Ray-Ban Meta makes sense when hands-free capture, calls, audio and AI assistance are central. XREAL One Pro makes sense when your main goal is a private large display for games, movies or work.

They are not substitutes

Buying one because it wins a generic “smart glasses” ranking can be misleading. The absence or presence of a display and camera changes the entire job the product performs.
