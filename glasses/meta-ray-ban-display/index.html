What kind of smart glasses are these?

Meta Ray-Ban Display is categorized by GlassesDB as HUD glasses. This matters because products sold as “smart glasses” can behave very differently: some prioritize cameras and AI, some show compact information in your field of view, and others act mainly as wearable displays.

What to check before buying
