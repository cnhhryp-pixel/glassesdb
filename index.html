Smart glasses questions, answered.

Do they need a phone?

Sometimes. It depends on whether the product uses a companion app, a video source or onboard processing.

Do they all have cameras?

No. Many HUD and display glasses are deliberately camera-free.

Good for gaming?

Display glasses can be—if your source device and video connection are compatible.

AR vs smart glasses?

Smart glasses is the broad category; AR is one visual subset.
